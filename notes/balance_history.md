# Cobblemon Delta Ranked Balance / Patch / Meta History

## Pre-New Season Main Team Development
The first successful main team shell was built around Tinkaton-Gamma, Gliscor, Corviknight, Ogerpon-Wellspring, Secret Armor, and Tangled Time. Later versions rotated Scizor-Mega, Draculedge, Delta Marshadow, Shrookle, and Garganacl.

## Battle Point / Economy Phase
Key economy decisions: hundo Landorus-Therian with Intimidate for 1.25m looked good but was later sold. Tangled Time was around 1.8m–2m for ~81–82% IV. Secret Armor was historically around 500k but paid 1.5m summon due to rarity and BP-capped to 94%. Heatran-Delta was 800k plus 800–1100 BP, while regular Heatran could be cheaper if Flash Fire avoided BP. Iron Sentinel at 6.5m was too much; at 1m it became worth testing.

## Tangled Time / Upgrade Clarification
Mods clarified that Upgrade is coded as Booster Energy replacement because Booster Energy sprite was missing. Upgrade boosts Tangled Time’s ability, not its signature move. The boost may display as “heightened” without a normal +1 stat stage.

## Draculedge Reward Phase
Draculedge became available at 300 ranked battles. Early GTS value was around 9m due to reward rarity, expected to drop once more players reach 300 battles. Shadow Sneak was preferred over Thunderbolt for priority.

## New Season Patch Reaction
Sleep became worse, making Shrookle’s Spore set less attractive. Shrookle shifted from Sticky Web / Sludge Bomb / Spore / Toxic Spikes to Sticky Web / Rapid Spin / Leech Seed / Toxic. Thunder Punch mention on Tinkaton-Gamma was corrected to Thunder Wave, and Defog remained important in later builds.

## High Ladder Mega Lopunny / Gholdengo-Delta Problem
At Top 20, the user repeatedly faced the #3/#1 player with Mega Lopunny Scrappy, Gholdengo-Delta Z-crystal, Skarmory, Molteon, Delta Steelix, and Tapukehe. Mega Lopunny always led. If Shrookle led, Lopunny U-turned into Gholdengo-Delta and punished Web. If anything else led, Lopunny outspeed/OHKO pressure was huge. Lesson: do not destroy a good team for one matchup, but avoid predictable Web leads and lack of contact punish.

## Rain Meta Development
A friend’s rain used Barraskewda, Urshifu-Rapid, Ogerpon-Wellspring, Pelipper, Mega Swampert, and Delta Cyndaquil/Typhlosion with Drizzle. Issue: too physical-heavy. Iron Sentinel changed discussion because Forecast Warning executes immediately in heavy rain. Recommended direction became Pelipper / Urshifu-Rapid / Ogerpon-Wellspring / Iron Sentinel / Delta Typhlosion / Ferrothorn.

## Team Building Service Phase
User started offering ranked team building services through town Mesa. Credentials: consistently Top 100 ranked player and creator/dev of Delta-Calc on Modrinth. Pricing proposed: Basic Review 40k, Full Team 100k, Full Team + Battle Guide 200k.

## First Order: Terapagos-ATOM + Tangled Time
Customer wanted Atom Terapagos, Tangled Time, no budget, future Atom Gallade, maybe Girashadow and Delta Marshadow. Customer planned to get the Z-move for Atom Terapagos, so report had to distinguish Astral Burst vs Universe Expansion. Delta Marshadow moves and Regenerator changed the write-up toward offensive pivot role.

## Second Order: Archapult Aggressive Team
Customer wanted Archapult, aggressive team, tank pivot, budget under 10m each. Archapult does not get Earthquake. Original build included Kingambit, but customer disliked too many Steel types and too many physical mons. Clefgar was chosen to replace Kingambit as special utility/defensive glue.

## Third Order: Kyurem Hyper Offense
Customer requested hyper offense using Kyurem and maybe Iron Valiant/Valiant Time with no budget constraints. Direction: Kyurem breaker, Valiant/Valiant Time fast pressure, hazard/screen lead, multiple speed-control options, and anti-Steel/Fairy measures.

## 2026-05-22 Mid-Season Patch
Source: `reference/mid-season-changes-2026-05-22.txt` and `reference/season-7-z-moves.txt`.

### Nerfs
- **Draculedge** 144/96 → 132/108 Atk/SpDef. BST 600 unchanged. Twin Cross still mixed-friendly but slightly less Banded; SpDef-leaning bulk on a Conviction breaker is a small DDance / special-attacker shift.
- **Spiritomb-Unbound** Spectre Onslaught secondary strikes deal 10% → **7.5%** of the original. Multi-hit damage drops noticeably (Poltergeist/Pursuit total drops from 1.3x to 1.225x of base).
- **Klinklang-Delta line** loses Earth Power, gains Trick. Choice Specs Trick is the new utility line; Heatran/-Delta and Garganacl no longer eat a free Earth Power.
- **Iron Pilot** 124/136/56 → 118/122/76 Atk/SpA/SpDef. BST 590. Less raw nuke, more bulk on the special side.

### Buffs
- **Snorlax-Delta (Cherry)** Cherry Blessing now applies Leech Seed; if the target is already Seeded, it inflicts Drowsy. Big utility upgrade — Seed + Drowsy on the same move is a wincon enabler.
- **Magoodra** HP/Def/SpDef +8/+12/+8, Atk/SpA/Spe -12/-8/-8. BST 560. Now a true physical wall, less mixed offensive presence. Likely climbs from A- back toward A.
- **Iron Sentinel** Forecast Warning fires immediately in **any weather** (was Rain only), and if it fires immediately the move's BP rises to **130**. Iron Sentinel goes from rain-only specialist to a flex pick on any weather team — sun, sand, snow all enable the immediate 130 BP nuke.
- **Apex Tyrant** gains U-Turn. Real pivot now.
- **Striding Sun** HP 61 → **111** (+50), Atk +18, Spe +12. BST 590 → **670**. Gains Dragon Dance. Outrage replaced with **Helios Storm** (Dragon physical, 100 BP, 100%, ignores ignorable abilities — bypasses Multiscale, Lightning Rod, etc.). **Banned in ranked and gyms** (see below).
- **Iron Moon** HP +18, SpA +18, **Spe 74 → 120** (+46). BST 590 → **670**. Gains Focus Blast. Steel Beam replaced with **Selene Pulse** (Steel special, 100 BP, 100%, ignores ignorable abilities). **Banned in ranked and gyms.**

### New Z-Moves (S7)
Source: `reference/season-7-z-moves.txt`. Each requires a species-specific Z-crystal and a specific required move.
- **Azumarill-Delta** — Deltaazumarillium Z + Icicle Flail → **Glacial Pinion** (Ice physical, 210 BP). Inflicts damage and **traps the target** as long as the user remains in.
- **Drifblimp** — Drifblimpium Z + Phantasmal Voyage → **One-way Journey** (Ghost special, 175 BP). Burns the user on use.
- **Hydrapple-Ultra** — Ultrahydrapplium Z + Syrup Bomb → **Capricious Syrup Cannonade** (Grass special, 175 BP). Sets **Sticky Web** on use.
- **Glimmora-Ultra** — Ultraglimmorium Z + Hyperspace Hole → **Wormhole Pillar** (Psychic special, 175 BP). Changes the user's ability to **Adaptability** on use.
- **Zeraora-Delta** — Deltazeraorium Z + Flashpoint Fists → **Crimson Fist Blitz** (Fire physical, 180 BP). Changes the user's ability to **Iron Fist** on use (combos with the Flashpoint Fists → punching-move bug fix below).
- **Victini-Delta** — Deltavictinium Z + V-ascend → **Vow To Win** (Electric status). Raises the user's **Attack, Special Attack, and Speed by +1**.
- **Mewrai** — Mewrainium Z + New Moon → **A Thousand Moons** (Dark special, 185 BP). Clears hazards on **both sides** of the field.

### ZA-Mega Abilities Implemented
Mega Delphox, Mega Dragonite, Mega Meganium, Mega Feraligatr, Mega Skarmory, Mega Emboar, and Mega Chandelure now use their ZA abilities in-mod. Note Mega Delphox is still banned per `banlist.md`.

### New Bans (ranked + gyms)
- Striding Sun (post-buff, BST 670)
- Iron Moon (post-buff, BST 670)
- Gardevoir-ATOM, Gallade-ATOM — temporary, until their obtainment method ships.

### Bug Fixes
- Prospect no longer grants the SpDef boost when a future move fails (was a silent boost on miss/immunity).
- **Flashpoint Fists is now a punching move** — interacts with Iron Fist (Zeraora-Delta's Z-move changes ability to Iron Fist, so this matters), Punching Glove, and Mega Heracross / Mega Lopunny boosts.
