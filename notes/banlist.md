## Quick Sanity Checks
- Base Kyurem is legal. All three fused Kyurem forms (Black, White, Original) are banned.
- Gholdengo is banned. **Gholdengo-Delta is legal** (Water type, Magic Bounce, the Z-Ride The Wave threat).
- Urshifu-Single-Strike is banned. **Urshifu-Rapid-Strike is legal** (the rain breaker).
- Ogerpon-Hearthflame is banned. **Ogerpon-Wellspring, Ogerpon-Cornerstone, and base Ogerpon are legal** unless flagged elsewhere.
- Landorus-Incarnate is banned. **Landorus-Therian is legal**.
- Both Mega Mewtwo forms and Mewthree are banned. Base Mewtwo is also banned.
- Both Zacian and Zamazenta forms (Hero and Crowned) are banned.
- Marshadow is banned. **Delta Marshadow is legal** (Fire/Ghost Regenerator pivot).
- Mega Lucario and Mega Lucario-Z are both banned. Base Lucario is legal.
- Mewtwo Megas, Blastoise-Mega, Alakazam-Mega, Kangaskhan-Mega, Blaziken-Mega, Raichu-Mega-Y, Delphox-Mega, Greninja-Mega are all banned. Most other Megas (Scizor, Lopunny, Diancie, Banette, Chandelure, etc.) are legal.

## When To Apply
Before locking any S/A+ recommendation, grep this file for the species name. If it appears under "Currently Banned," do not include it in a build, a replacement option, or a battle doc matchup answer. Treat the legal sibling forms (Delta variants, alt formes) as separate entries unless explicitly listed here.
