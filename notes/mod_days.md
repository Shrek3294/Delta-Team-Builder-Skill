# Cobblemon Delta Ranked Pokémon Notes

## Tinkaton-Gamma
Specially defensive utility glue. It gives Dragon immunity, Defog, Moonlight recovery, Fairy pressure, weather disruption, and Delta Stream support through Parasol Prayer. Parasol Prayer sets Delta Stream, overwrites weather, and reduces Flying-type weakness.

```txt
Tinkaton-Gamma @ Leftovers
Ability: Dazzling -> Parasol Prayer
Calm Nature
EVs: 252 HP / 4 Def / 252 SpD
- Updraft
- Moonblast
- Defog
- Moonlight
```

Thunder Wave was tested over Defog, but Defog became preferred when the team needed hazard control. Do not casually replace this slot if your team relies on Dragon immunity and weather control.

## Eruptois
Fire/Water style breaker, but struggled in practice because its damage output relies on HP-dependent moves. When switching into attacks, it often dropped to around 50% HP before moving, which made its HP-based moves weak. It needs pivot support, speed control, and safe entry.

## Draculedge
Dragon/Fighting ranked reward attacker. Stats (post 2026-05-22 nerf): 64 HP / **132** Atk / 76 Def / 120 SpA / **108** SpD / 100 Spe. BST 600. Ability Conviction deals 25% more damage on super-effective moves. Twin Cross is a Dragon physical 50 BP move that hits twice with burn/paralysis chances.

Shadow Sneak is usually preferred over Thunderbolt because priority matters a ton on ladder. Thunderbolt only makes sense if bulky Waters/Flyings are a repeated problem.

Post-nerf note: -12 Atk pushes the kit slightly toward mixed or special — SpDef +12 makes it bulkier on the SpDef side, so it eats a Moonblast or Surf a bit better but Banded raw damage drops. DDance + Twin Cross still works; if you wanted Choice Band it's now a softer click.

## Iron Sentinel
Water/Flying. Quark Drive. Stats: 86 HP / 90 Atk / 84 Def / 118 SpA / 98 SpD / 114 Spe. Forecast Warning is Flying special 120 BP, does nothing on selection then hits two turns later — but **as of 2026-05-22** executes immediately in **any weather** (was Rain only), and when it does fire immediately the BP rises to **130**.

Post-buff: this is now a flex pick on any weather team — Sun (Charizard-Y / Torkoal), Sand (Tyranitar / Hippowdon), Snow, or Rain all enable immediate 130 BP nuke. The rain pairing is still the most consistent setup, but Sun pairings open up (Charizard-Y + Iron Sentinel + Iron Moth special core is now real). Outside any weather, the two-turn delay still exists, so it remains soft to weather denial (Parasol Prayer Tinkaton-Gamma still neutralizes it specifically). Likely climbs in viability — re-evaluate as the meta settles.

## Gholdengo-Delta
Pure Water type, Magic Bounce ability (the only ability listed). Stats: 87 HP / 76 Atk / 75 Def / 133 SpA / 99 SpD / 80 Spe (BST 550). Pearl Chest Pokemon, Summer Event obtain. Source: Vik_Works.

Signature move **Pearly Parade**: Water special, 120 BP, 100% accuracy, lowers user's SpA by 1 after hitting. Like Draco Meteor but Water. Hits all adjacent in doubles.

Standard set: Pearly Parade / Shadow Ball / Focus Blast / Recover @ Deltagholdium Z, Timid, 252 SpA / 4 SpD / 252 Spe. Recover keeps it alive as a Magic Bounce glue piece all game. High-ladder Z-crystal threat paired with Mega Lopunny. Punishes hazard leads and Web attempts.

## Gholdaton
Fairy/Steel Tinkaton plus Gholdengo style fusion. Base: 92/92/80/80/120/96 BST 560. Abilities: Own Tempo, Good As Gold, **Surge Surfer**. A-tier per April 29 viability.

Confirmed 1500+ set (live usage):

Surge Surfer (62.16% usage) doubles Speed in Electric Terrain, so the canonical pair is Tapu Koko (the 56.76% teammate), and at base 96 Speed that becomes 384 in-battle with Jolly. Fire/Poison Paradox special breaker. Base: 80/70/60/140/110/110 BST 570. Ability: **Quark Drive** (Electric Terrain or Booster boost). A-tier per April 29 viability and rank 25 at 1500+ usage.

EVs: 124 Def / 132 SpA / 252 Spe

## Hydrapple-Ultra

Poison/Dragon Hydrapple variant. Released to ranked **2026-05-10**. Bulky offensive pivot. Stats per Cob: -10 HP / -10 Atk / +10 Def / +10 SpA vs base Hydrapple. Resists U-turn (was weak), neutral to Fairy (was weak), but weak to Ground instead of resistant.

Regenerator is the consensus ability over Spoiled Goods. Spoiled Goods makes Giga Drain Poison-type and is *why* Hydrapple-Ultra doesn't get Leaf Storm distribution. AV Regen is "a bulky offensive pivot, not a wincon — use something else as the wincon" (beeg_lad). Nasty Plot Regen is the wallbreaker alt if a client wants it as the actual win condition.

**Damage cites from channel testing:**
- 252+ SpA Hydrapple-Ultra Sludge Bomb vs. 0 HP / 4 SpD Tapu Lele: 288-338 (102.4 - 120.2%) — guaranteed OHKO.
- 252 SpA Spoiled Goods Hydrapple-Ultra Giga Drain vs. 0 HP / 4 SpD Ogerpon-Wellspring: 306-360 (101.6 - 119.6%) — guaranteed OHKO.

**Walls / checks:** Kingambit "giga walls it" (Steel + Dark resists Poison and Dragon). Mega Mawile +2 Play Rough OHKOs uninvested Hydrapple-Ultra; even fully bulky 252+ Def variants only have a 31% chance to survive +2. Tapu Lele Specs Psyshock OHKOs through AV in Psychic Terrain. Great Tusk Headlong Rush is the physical answer (~81% OHKO chance after rocks vs base Hydrapple; AV-Ultra survives that better but still hates physical pressure).

**Risk flag:** the channel expects this gets nerfed. "It is going to be so funny when hydrapple ends up nerfed because people think it is too bulky with regen" (HeartUnderBlade); "invin gets paid off by big wellspring, so unfortunately u-hydrapple is on a timer." Don't lock long-term builds around it without flagging the volatility to the client.

**S7 Z-move (2026-05-22):** Ultrahydrapplium Z + Syrup Bomb → **Capricious Syrup Cannonade** (Grass special, 175 BP). Inflicts damage and sets **Sticky Web** on use. Free hazard setup on a wallbreaker — turns Hydrapple-Ultra into a viable HO lead alternative (click the Z turn 1 for damage + Web, then pivot or keep mashing). Note this means the AV Regen pivot set wants Modest 252 SpA and Syrup Bomb in the slot if running the Z-crystal — Assault Vest is incompatible with Z-crystal so it's a different role entirely. Don't try to share the same Hydrapple-Ultra across both roles.

## 2026-05-22 Patch Additions
Below are mons newly relevant after the mid-season patch. Treat as preliminary — sets settle as the ladder digests the changes. Source: `reference/mid-season-changes-2026-05-22.txt`, `reference/season-7-z-moves.txt`.

### Snorlax-Delta (Cherry)
Cherry Blessing now applies **Leech Seed** to the target; if the target is already Seeded, it inflicts **Drowsy**. Massive utility upgrade — Seed + Drowsy on one move is wincon glue (forces sleep next turn or a switch into Seed chip). Pairs naturally with hazards and slow Regen pivots. Re-evaluate viability — was niche, this is a real role now.

### Magoodra
Stats post-buff (2026-05-22): **76** HP / **100** Atk / **152** Def / **108** SpA / **68** SpD / **56** Spe. BST 560. Pure physical wall now — +20 physical bulk total, -28 offense + speed total. Likely climbs from A- toward A; the Magoodra entry on the April 29 tierlist (A-) is now stale to the upside. Default to Curse + Recover + Body Press / Iron Defense style physical wall sets.

### Iron Pilot
Stats post-nerf (2026-05-22): 78 HP / **118** Atk / 84 Def / **122** SpA / **76** SpD / 112 Spe. BST 590. Less raw nuke (mixed offense both shave), more SpDef bulk (+20). Likely steady at A; the mixed Quark Drive breaker role still exists, just slightly softer. Booster Energy / Upgrade Quark Drive sets unchanged in shape.

### Klinklang-Delta line
Loses **Earth Power**, gains **Trick**. The Choice Specs Trick line is now the utility option — locks an opposing wall into Leftovers/Boots while Klinklang-Delta keeps clicking STAB. Heatran, Heatran-Delta, and Garganacl no longer eat a free Earth Power; route around them with teammates rather than expecting Klinklang-Delta to one-shot through Ground coverage.

### Apex Tyrant
Gains **U-Turn**. Real pivot now — was a slower setup breaker, can now click U-Turn into a teammate without committing. Pairs with Regenerator cores (Alomomola, Marshadow-Delta).

### Striding Sun, Iron Moon
**Both banned in ranked and gyms** post-buff. See `banlist.md`. Do not include in any build, replacement option, or matchup answer. Striding Sun's Helios Storm (Dragon physical 100 BP ability-ignoring) and Iron Moon's Selene Pulse (Steel special 100 BP ability-ignoring) are noted here in case the user asks about Ubers tournament context — that ruleset doesn't follow ranked bans, so they may be legal there.

### Azumarill-Delta
**S7 Z-move:** Deltaazumarillium Z + Icicle Flail → **Glacial Pinion** (Ice physical, 210 BP). Inflicts damage and **traps the target** as long as the user remains in. Trapping on a 210 BP nuke is a real wincon enabler — pick off a chipped sweeper or break a defensive pivot. Belly Drum sets become more interesting since the trap turn buys setup against the trapped mon.

### Drifblimp
**S7 Z-move:** Drifblimpium Z + Phantasmal Voyage → **One-way Journey** (Ghost special, 175 BP). Burns the user on use. Self-burn means built-in Flame Orb activation without the prep — interesting on a Flare Boost / Heatproof body or as a one-time damage spike followed by a switch. Cost: burned for the rest of the match, so usually a one-shot.

### Glimmora-Ultra
**S7 Z-move:** Ultraglimmorium Z + Hyperspace Hole → **Wormhole Pillar** (Psychic special, 175 BP). Changes the user's ability to **Adaptability** on use. After the Z, every subsequent STAB hits at 2x. This is the most interesting of the new Zs — turns Glimmora-Ultra into a permanent post-Z Adaptability wallbreaker. Pair with hazard support and click the Z early.

### Zeraora-Delta
**S7 Z-move:** Deltazeraorium Z + Flashpoint Fists → **Crimson Fist Blitz** (Fire physical, 180 BP). Changes the user's ability to **Iron Fist** on use. Combos directly with the **Flashpoint Fists punching-move fix** in the same patch — post-Z, Flashpoint Fists itself benefits from Iron Fist's 1.2x punch boost. So the Z is a 180 BP nuke + an ongoing Iron Fist boost to every punching move (Close Combat is not a punch move, but Power-Up Punch, Plasma Fists, Drain Punch, Mach Punch and Flashpoint Fists itself are). Build around physical Zeraora-Delta with multiple punch moves.

### Victini-Delta
**S7 Z-move:** Deltavictinium Z + V-ascend → **Vow To Win** (Electric status). Raises Atk, SpA, **and Speed by +1** in one click. Mixed +1 boost is rare and serious — Victini-Delta with a Z setup + V-ascend STAB + coverage is a real win condition. Already A- on the April 29 list; this almost certainly climbs.
