# Cobblemon Delta Ranked Team Building Principles

## Start With The Win Conditions
Every team needs 1–3 realistic ways to win. Good examples include Ogerpon-Wellspring after Swords Dance, Draculedge with priority and Conviction pressure, Tangled Time with Upgrade and Calm Mind, Mega Scizor with Swords Dance + Bullet Punch, Archapult forcing progress, Terapagos-ATOM with Zenith Storm/Z-move pressure, Iron Coil under Trick Room, and Iron Sentinel in rain.

## Do Not Stack Too Much Physical Offense
Barraskewda, Urshifu-Rapid, Ogerpon-Wellspring, and Mega Swampert are all scary, but together they invite the same answers: Skarmory, Corviknight, Alomomola, Rocky Helmet, Will-O-Wisp, Intimidate, and physical resist cycling. Fix with special breaker, status/chip, Knock Off, hazards, or mixed attackers.

## Defensive Glue Must Do Something
A defensive Pokémon should provide at least two of: hazards, removal, Knock Off, pivoting, weather control, status, recovery, immunities, priority denial, setup denial, or scouting. Tinkaton-Gamma, Gliscor, Corviknight, Clefgar, Ferrothorn, and Garganacl are good examples.

## Always Respect Speed Control
Speed control can be natural Speed, priority, Sticky Web, Trick Room, Choice Scarf, Booster/Upgrade-style boosts, Thunder Wave, weather speed, or priority like Bullet Punch/Sucker Punch/Shadow Sneak.

## Do Not Rely On One Lead Plan
If your whole plan is “lead Shrookle and click Web,” good players punish it. Identify anti-leads, have a plan if hazards fail, lead pivots sometimes, and use hazards midgame if needed.

## Weather Teams Need Non-Weather Value
Rain is strong, but if every mon only works in rain, the team collapses when weather is overwritten, Pelipper dies, Tinkaton-Gamma sets Delta Stream, or Terapagos-ATOM neutralizes weather/terrain. Use rain abusers plus glue.

## Economy Is Part Of Team Building
Ask whether the mon only works in one archetype, whether price will fall, whether it needs BP, whether the ability is correct, whether it can resell, and whether a cheaper alternative exists. Iron Sentinel at 6.5m is questionable; at 1m it is a good rain test. Draculedge at 9m may depreciate but is competitively real.

## Custom Mechanics Need Confirmation
Do not assume vanilla logic. Upgrade works as Booster Energy replacement. Tangled Time’s boost is ability-based. Ivy Cudgel should be Water type for Wellspring. Terapagos-ATOM Z-move choice changes form and ability implications. Parasol Prayer sets Delta Stream and overwrites weather.

## Use Role Compression To Build Around Favorites
When a client wants Archapult, ask what it beats, what beats it, what pivots it in, what removes hazards, what checks faster threats, what physical/special balance is needed, and what the backup win condition is.

## Write Battle Docs Around How The Team Actually Wins
Include team identity, each role, standard leads, when not to lead the default lead, early/mid/endgame plan, win conditions, what to preserve, common matchup guide, replacement options, and known weaknesses. Avoid vague advice and do not leave removed Pokémon like Kingambit referenced in the doc.

## Preserve Key Defensive Immunities
Removing a Pokémon can remove the only Dragon immunity, Ground immunity, or Water immunity. A stronger mon on paper can make the team worse if it breaks the defensive map.

## Top-Ladder Matchups Are Lessons, Not Always Rules
Losing badly to the #1 player does not mean the team is trash. It may mean they have specific anti-team structure, sequence better, or your lead pattern is predictable. Fix the structural issue without destroying a good team for one opponent.

## Item Choice Should Match Role
Leftovers for long-term durability, Rocky Helmet for contact punish, Toxic Orb on Poison Heal Clefgar, Upgrade on Booster-style custom users, Wellspring Mask on Ogerpon-Wellspring, Scizorite on Mega Scizor, and Terapagos Z-crystal only when exact Z-move plan is clear.

## Build Archetype Cores, Not Just Individual Sets
Rain core: setter + abuser + special breaker + Ferrothorn glue. Trick Room core: setter + setter + slow breaker + slow breaker + emergency speed. Balance core: wincon + wincon + glue + rocks + removal + speed control. HO core: lead + breaker + setup + setup + revenge + anti-stall.
