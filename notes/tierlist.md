## Tiering Philosophy

This is a practical ladder tier list, not a pure theoretical one. A Pokemon ranks highly if it performs well into common ranked structures, has role compression, is not overly matchup-fishy, has reliable sets, is worth its price/BP investment, contributes to real win conditions, and fits multiple teams or is elite in one archetype.

## Notable Reads From The April 29 Snapshot

**S-tier diversity is broad.** 15 mons covering every role: physical breakers (Great Tusk, Kingambit, Ogerpon-W), physical walls (Corviknight, Gliscor, Mega Scizor), special breakers (Dragapult, Iron Valiant, Tangled Time, Heatran-Delta), pivots (Marshadow-Delta), glue (Alomomola, Tinkaton-Gamma), setup wincon (Volcarona), terrain (none in S — Tapu Lele dropped to A, Tapu Koko in A). No single archetype dominates.

**Iron Valiant is S.** Confirmed as a default ladder pick. Mention it as a replacement option in any HO build.

**Heatran-Delta is S, regular Heatran is A.** Both are live, Heatran-Delta is the elite option.

**Tinkaton-Gamma is S.** Stays S despite Delta Stream being situational. Dragon immunity + Defog + Moonblast + Parasol Prayer is the role compression.

**Marshadow-Delta is S.** Regenerator offensive pivot is the canonical set per `mod_days.md`.

**Tapu Lele dropped to A, Tapu Koko sits in A** (Koko higher on usage at 1500+). Both work, default to Koko at top ladder.

**Roaring Moon is A.** Dark physical threat. Plan around it on every build.

**Spectreon is A-, Dragascue is A, Hatteon is A.** All real picks per the April 29 ranking. Spectreon is below where the live usage suggests (1500+ usage has it at 10.16%).

**Slaughtermaus-Mega is A** (rank 72 in source). The unmega'd Slaughtermaus is A- at rank 73. High usage at 1500+ but tiered below the top S/A picks; treat as a strong specific pick rather than a default.

**Draculedge is A** (ranked-reward attacker). Conviction + Twin Cross + Shadow Sneak still meta.

**Terapagos is A, Terapagos-ATOM is A.** ATOM is not above standard Terapagos in this ranking despite the 700 BST. ATOM is an investment piece, not a "must own."

**Custom forms with real viability:** Lopunny-ATOM (A), Gardevoir-ATOM-Mega (A), Mew-ATOM (A-), Pachirisu-ATOM (A-). Most other ATOM forms are B+ or lower.

**Fusion forms in S/A:** Tangled Time, Marshadow-Delta, Heatran-Delta, Tinkaton-Gamma in S; Gholdengo-Delta, Meowscarada-Delta, Samurott-Hisui, Slowking-Galar, Latias-Delta, Hatteon, Dragascue, Greninja-Omega-Ash, Uxie-Delta, Flygon-Ultra, Klinklang-Delta, Diancette, Tapu Kehe, Spiritomb-Unbound, Gholdaton, Azelf-Delta, Lucaurus, Staraptor-Delta in A. Many ladder mons are custom fusions, not vanilla pieces.

## Sanity Checks Before Recommending

1. **Is it banned?** Grep `notes/banlist.md`.
2. **What tier?** Default to S/A/A- for serious builds. B and below need a justified niche.
3. **Live usage?** Cross-check `notes/usage_stats.md`. A mon at S tier with low real usage is a flag (either a counter-meta pick or an under-appreciated piece).
4. **Confirmed set?** Check `notes/mod_days.md` for confirmed competitive sets before quoting one.
