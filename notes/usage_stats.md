## Read Of The Current Meta (1500+)

**The top 5 is the meta core.** Ogerpon-Wellspring at 28.85% is the single most common piece, paired against or alongside Corviknight (28.12%) and Great Tusk (27.46%). Any team that does not have a clean answer to all three is undertuned for ladder. Kingambit at 23.55% and Tinkaton-Gamma at 23.37% round out the top 5 as the meta-defining bulky/cleanup pieces.

**Steel walls are universal.** Corviknight, Tinkaton-Gamma, Heatran-Delta, Heatran (vanilla), Ferrothorn, and Scizor all sit in the top 20. Six different Steel walls in heavy use. Anything that can't break Steels is going to struggle on ladder. This is the single most actionable read: builds need real Steel coverage (Fire moves, Fighting moves, Knock + chip, or Magnet Pull tech).

**Both Heatrans are live.** Heatran-Delta at 18.37% (rank 7) and vanilla Heatran at 14.93% (rank 16) are both common. Be specific in builds about which one is being recommended; they have different roles.

**Tapu Koko has displaced Tapu Lele on raw usage.** Tapu Koko is rank 9 at 18.09%. Tapu Lele does not appear in the top 45 of this 1500+ snapshot. Lele still works as a Specs breaker, but at the top of the ladder Koko is the more played terrain setter (Electric Terrain, Volt Switch tempo, Specs/Scarf flexibility).

**Charizard at 17.75% is real.** Almost certainly Mega Charizard-Y given Drought + sun pressure. The 03/03/2026 viability dropped Mega Charizard-Y from S to A but it stayed popular.

**Slaughtermaus at 16.23%.** Not in notes anywhere. Need to research the set, ability, and role for the next build. Probably a custom/fusion mon based on the naming pattern.

**Iron Valiant at 16.12%.** Previously flagged in notes as "needs more data." It's clearly a top piece in the current meta. Treat as A-tier minimum.

**Marshadow-Delta at 11.17%.** Confirms the S-tier placement in notes. Regenerator offensive pivot is genuinely meta.

**Dragapult at 18.13%, Roaring Moon at 8.25%.** Two real Dark/Dragon threats. Psychic-stacked teams (like the recent xayah build) have to plan around both.

**Hazard ecosystem is alive.** Great Tusk (rocks + spin), Glimmora (rocks + sticky web threat), Ferrothorn (spikes), Shrookle (web), Gliscor (rocks). Hazard removal slots are mandatory.

**Rain core still common.** Pelipper at 7.64%, Ferrothorn at 15.52%, Swampert (Mega) at 9.33%. Less dominant than at the start of the season but still a real ladder structure.

## Implications For Building

1. **Default Steel coverage is required.** Every build needs at least one real answer to Corviknight + Tinkaton-Gamma + a Heatran variant. Fire STAB, Fighting STAB, Magnet Pull, or strong Knock chip.
2. **Default Ground answer is required.** Great Tusk at 27.46% and Landorus-T at 19.81% means Levitate, Flying typing, or a fast Grass/Water resist is mandatory.
3. **Default Dark answer is required.** Kingambit (23.55%), Spiritomb-Unbound (8.50%), Roaring Moon (8.25%), plus Dragapult Ghost coverage. Fairy STAB and Fighting STAB are the canonical answers.
4. **Hazard removal is non-negotiable.** Boots cover the entry math but you still want active removal (Spin or Defog).
5. **Iron Valiant is a default speed-control / setup option** at 16% usage. Mention it in replacement options for setup-style builds.
6. **Tapu Koko is the default terrain pick at top ladder.** Tapu Lele is still legal and strong but recognize Koko is more common at 1500+.
