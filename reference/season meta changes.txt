# Analysis:
<:cobblemonyellow:1117423379755057283> Trick Room and Sun were heavily nerfed last patch. Thus, all their abusers are automatically less viable. I still think they're very strong playstyles but they're not as brainless as they used to be. Simply having Magoodra bring in any of its dozens of sun abusers or Magirigus 100% guaranteeing Trick Room with an extra turn is no longer a thing.
Some of them will remain in their tiers just due to how strong they are in general like Ursaluna.
<:cobblemonyellow:1117423379755057283> Signature Z-Moves are unbanned and as such some Pokemon just have a direct advantage over others. Z-Moves are an offensive tools so defensive cores need to take them into consideration unless they want to get blown up by Tangled Time or Gholdengo-Delta. There will be new Signature Z-Moves added to exisitng Pokemon coming with the Ultra Space update so look forward to that!
<:cobblemonyellow:1117423379755057283> Mega Raichu-Y and Mega Delphox are just incredibely hard to wall or revenge-kill due to their absurd speed-tier, coverage, and set-up. It's not something worth placing in the tier
<:cobblemonyellow:1117423379755057283> Some players brought up that they are UU in the Legends-ZA OU meta, but that is a tier with no concern of defensive Pokemon and is a 90% HO meta with specific defensive checks for specific Pokemon. It's a fun, but not healthy metagame and that's why you'll see these powerhouses going in a low tier like UU. You also need to take into consideration that a lot of their DLC Mega Pokemon have amazing abilities that set them at a level way higher than that of the server's. I won't be revoking the ban on either of them especially now that Delphox also has Levitate guaranteed as an ability.
